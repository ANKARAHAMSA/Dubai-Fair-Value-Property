# UAE Data Science & ML Engineering Interview Prep (`DubaiFairValue`)

Ten realistic technical & domain interview questions grounded directly in `DubaiFairValue`'s architecture, codebase, and verified metrics.

---

### 1. Why did you forbid random train/test splits and enforce strict temporal splitting + future-deletion invariance?
- **Answer:** Real estate prices exhibit strong temporal autocorrelation and cyclical regime shifts (`2020` dip -> `2021–2024` rally). A random split leaks future neighborhood medians and post-cutoff market momentum backward into training rows, artificially inflating accuracy. In [`src/dfv/features/build.py`](../src/dfv/features/build.py), trailing `3m/6m/12m` area, project, segment, and yield features use strictly prior sales (`instance_date < t`). [`tests/unit/test_leakage.py`](../tests/unit/test_leakage.py) asserts both strict chronological disjointness (`max_train < min_val < min_test`) and exact invariance of past rows when future transactions are deleted.

### 2. Why did you add Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) on top of LightGBM instead of trusting raw quantile predictions?
- **Answer:** Uncalibrated gradient-boosted quantile models (`alpha = 0.05, 0.95`) routinely mis-cover on future test slices due to distribution shift and overfitting. In [`src/dfv/models/intervals.py`](../src/dfv/models/intervals.py), split-conformal calibration computes signed conformity scores $E_i = \max(\hat{q}_{0.05}(x_i) - y_i,\; y_i - \hat{q}_{0.95}(x_i))$ on a strictly separated `28,539`-row calibration period (`2023-11-21` to `2024-02-22`, strictly disjoint from `val_tune` ending `2023-11-20`). Adjusting quantile predictions by the finite-sample quantile of $E_i$ brought empirical 90% test-set coverage to **`89.93%`** on `70,608` future sales (and `91.67%` in sample mode), preserving heteroscedastic widths without ad-hoc multipliers.

### 3. Your spatial (unseen-project) hold-out MdAPE jumped from `6.94%` to `20.22%`. Doesn't that mean the model failed?
- **Answer:** On the contrary — surfacing that degradation honestly in [`docs/model_card.md`](model_card.md) proves our validation caught structural limitations instead of hiding behind in-project temporal error. Holding out `680` unseen projects (while recomputing area/segment trailing priors strictly excluding held-out project sales) removes project-level trailing medians (`project_trailing_*`), and the public DLD fallback dataset carries area-centroid coordinates and `100% Unknown` developer labels until `dld_projects-open` is approved. For brand-new launches lacking prior transactions or project coordinates, relying solely on community-level priors creates genuine epistemic uncertainty.

### 4. Why did you switch architectures between the full DLD run (`lightgbm_tuned`) and the small sample bundle (`ResidualBoostedLGBM`, ADR-008)?
- **Answer:** Empirical benchmarking showed opposite trade-offs by sample scale. On `459,145` real DLD rows, standalone Optuna-tuned LightGBM outperformed a Ridge + Residual-Tree hybrid (`6.94%` vs `8.14%` MdAPE) because dense nonlinear location interactions dominate and a linear global date trend fit across 2021–2023 overshoots 2024 moderation. Conversely, on the `5,000`-row CI synthetic bundle, trees cannot extrapolate an upward time trend beyond the final training timestamp, whereas Stage-1 regularized Ridge captures smooth macro appreciation and Stage-2 residual LightGBM captures nonlinear location/metro terms (`6.45%` vs `6.72%` Ridge baseline, `7.27%` standalone LightGBM).

### 5. How do you convert log-space `TreeSHAP` values into dirham (`AED`) contributions cleanly, and what is the caveat?
- **Answer:** Because the model targets $y = \log(\text{AED/sqm})$ while stakeholders read total `AED`, naively multiplying `area_sqm * exp(phi_j)` violates additivity. In [`src/dfv/models/explain.py`](../src/dfv/models/explain.py), we compute Mean Value Theorem scaling across log SHAP values $\phi_j$: $\Delta_{\text{total}} = \text{area\_sqm} \cdot \left(\exp(\text{base} + \sum \phi_j) - \exp(\text{base})\right)$ and allocate $\Delta_{\text{total}}$ proportionally by $\phi_j / \sum \phi_k$ (exact in the first-order limit). **Caveat:** TreeSHAP attributions reflect conditional statistical model sensitivity across correlated features (like `area_trailing_12m_ppsqm` and `area_name`), not isolated causal effects.

### 6. Why did off-plan show `+28.27%` under `Area x Quarter FE` but only `+5.48%` under `Project x Quarter FE`?
- **Answer:** In [`src/dfv/analysis/developer_premium.py`](../src/dfv/analysis/developer_premium.py), comparing off-plan vs ready sales across an entire Dubai community (`Area x Quarter`) mixes newly launched luxury master developments against older ready towers nearby. Adding `Project x Quarter` fixed effects compares off-plan vs ready units inside the **same project and quarter**, shrinking the premium gap by 5x to `+5.48%`. Even that `+5.48%` remains an observational association partly capturing post-handover developer payment plans and brand-new developer warranties unavailable in DLD tabular columns.

### 7. Why did your Route 2020 Metro DiD analysis switch to additive Two-Way Fixed Effects and show `-4.72%` post-opening?
- **Answer:** Interacted `area x quarter` fixed effects absorb every area-level `Treated_a x Post_t` interaction cleanly when coordinates are only community centroids, leaving zero identifying variation (`null` coefficient). Switching to additive `Area + Quarter` Two-Way FE via alternating projections in [`src/dfv/analysis/_stats.py`](../src/dfv/analysis/_stats.py) recovers the DiD trajectory alongside pre-trend inspection. Crucially, Route 2020 opened in `2021` amidst COVID recovery and Expo-adjacent supply booms concentrated in outer residential corridors vs Prime Downtown/Marina controls, plus fallback coordinates are area centroids (`within_area_identifiable = false`), so we explicitly flag both in `metro_effect.json` rather than claiming causal uplift.

### 8. How do you make natural-language property search (`Ask the Data`) safe against prompt injection and SQL injection?
- **Answer:** The LLM never touches DuckDB SQL directly. In [`src/dfv/search/`](../src/dfv/search/), `schema.py` defines a strict Pydantic v2 `PropertyFilters` model (`extra="forbid"`, bounded numeric ranges, guarded result caps `<= 100`), `llm.py` enforces JSON-only schema extraction with pre-parse prompt-injection screening (`SearchGuardrailError`), single validation retry, and deterministic fallback to `rules.py` (`regex + rapidfuzz` area matching) when no API key exists. Finally, `executor.py` injects values solely via parameterized DuckDB `?` placeholders (`read_parquet`), and displays the exact `"I understood: ..."` summary back to the user.

### 9. What happens when a user asks the API to evaluate an unfamiliar community or thin market segment?
- **Answer:** Rather than hiding low support behind overconfident labels, [`src/dfv/models/verdict.py`](../src/dfv/models/verdict.py) checks segment sample counts (`trailing_count < 15`) and recognized area coverage. Thin or unknown segments return `verdict = "insufficient data"` with `confidence = "Low"` while still reporting the calibrated interval (`low <= estimate <= high`). In addition, `load_model_bundle` refuses FastAPI startup (`ModelMetadataError`) whenever `metadata.json` or required tracking fields (`model_version`, `git_sha`, `training_window`, `feature_list`) are missing.

### 10. How did you verify Dubai domain hygiene cleanly before letting features or models train?
- **Answer:** [`src/dfv/data/clean.py`](../src/dfv/data/clean.py) filters non-sale procedures (`mortgages`, `gifts`, `land`, `commercial`), standardizes bilingual area/metro variants via curated `name_map.csv` + `rapidfuzz`, flags exact duplicates, hard non-market bounds (`AED 1,500–250,000 / sqm`), and `|MAD z-score| > 3.5` statistical outliers (retaining `flag_outlier` transparently for exploration while excluding flagged rows from training). It then asserts macro UAE pricing hierarchies hold on recent medians (`Downtown Dubai AED 26.8k/sqm > Dubai South AED 10.9k/sqm`; `Palm Jumeirah AED 32.0k/sqm > Discovery Gardens AED 9.2k/sqm`), followed by strict Pandera schema validation (`validate.py`).
